### PR-005: L4 main-level sum auto-rebalancing not implemented (only parent-level)
- Status: Resolved
- Description: Spec section 3 rule 2 states "for each Parent with non-zero share, sum of its Main children = 100.0% of that Parent's share +/- 0.5%." Rule 4 says delta <= 0.5% triggers auto-rebalance "of the largest sibling at the same level under the same parent (i.e., a Main-layer violation under Parent Growth rebalances the largest Main under Growth only)." This violates the spec-mandated auto-rebalance behavior for the Main layer.
- Decision Reason: `rebalance_layer` now accepts optional `target` (default 100.0) and `layer` parameters. `categorize_one` iterates each parent, calls `rebalance_layer(mains, tolerance=0.5, target=expected_share, layer="main")`, and aggregates all rebalance log entries. Task 007 has three new tests: main-level rebalance with custom target, main-level exceeds-tolerance, and alphabetical tie-breaking.
